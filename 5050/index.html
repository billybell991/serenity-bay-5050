❓ How To Use This App

🎟️ Selling Tickets

Find the camper's site in the list — or type their site number (like B4) in the search box at the top. Tap their card, then tap Cash or eTransfer, then choose the dollar amount. The site will be marked ✅ Visited!

➕ Multiple Buyers at One Site

If more than one person at the same site wants to buy tickets, tap ➕ Add Extra on their site card. A new entry — labelled 📎 Extra @ [site] — will appear right below it with its own Cash / eTransfer buttons. You can add as many extras as needed. To remove one, tap the ✕ button on the extra card.

↩️ Made a Mistake?

No problem! Tap the Undo button next to any visited site to reset it back to unvisited. Then you can re-enter it correctly.

🚫 Do Not Bother Sites

Some sites are marked 🚫 Do Not Bother — these campers have asked not to be visited. They'll appear grayed out in the list.

📊 Check the Tally

Tap the Tally button at the bottom anytime to see the running totals — cash collected, eTransfer, gross raised, and what the 50/50 prize would be right now!

🗺️ Park Map

Tap the Map button to see the full campground map. Use two fingers to pinch and zoom in on any area!

📱 Add This App to Your Home Screen

On iPhone (use Safari):

1. Open this page in Safari
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (the square with an arrow pointing up ⬆️)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap Add — it'll appear just like a real app!

On Android (use Chrome):

1. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots in the top-right corner)
2. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
3. Tap Add — you're done!
