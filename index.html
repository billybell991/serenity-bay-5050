❓ How To Use This App

🎯 Pick a Draw

Tap a card to open it. 50/50 Draw is our weekly campground 50/50. Booze Basket is the season-long basket draw. Each one keeps its own tickets and totals.

💳 Selling Tickets

Inside a draw, find the camper's site in the list — or type their site number (like B4) in the search box. Tap their card, then tap Cash or eTransfer, then choose the dollar amount. The site is marked ✅ Visited!

📊 Tally & 🗺️ Map

Tap Tally anytime to see running totals, and Map to view the full campground map — pinch to zoom in on any area.

📱 Add This App to Your Home Screen

On iPhone (use Safari):

1. Open this page in Safari
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (the square with an arrow pointing up ⬆️)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap Add — it'll appear just like a real app!

On Android (use Chrome):

1. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots in the top-right corner)
2. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
3. Tap Add — you're done!
